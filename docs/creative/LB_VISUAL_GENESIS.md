## V1 — Exploration

**Original purpose.** Fork the visual language three ways and choose. Three
desktop directions with mobile counterparts: **Monochrome Editorial (A)**,
**Warm Modern Luxury (B)**, **Experimental Future (C)**.

**Prompt intent.** Establish "Modern Frontier" as a register distinct from both
heritage western and generic DTC — and prove it could be done three ways before
committing.

**Visual vocabulary.** Ink Black `#0A0A0A`, Bone White `#F5F2EE`, Tobacco Leather
`#734F36`, Sandstone `#D9C5B2`, Indigo `#1B2B45`. Libre Caslon Text + Inter.
Macro material photography — a thumb on a pearl snap, hand-stitched tooled
leather. Nav: Women · Men · Custom · Wholesale.

**Important screens.** Direction A (pearl-snap macro, denim weave, workshop
bench, **the Dallas skyline**); Direction B (tooled leather, *"Tobacco & Indigo —
the essential textures of the modern west"*, product cards with material
descriptors).

**Strongest concepts.**
- **The research matrix with an explicit Reject column.** Seven references, each
  with Borrow / Reject / *"Reinterpretation for L&B"*. The most methodologically
  sound artefact in the entire corpus.
- **Experience modes Cinema / Balanced / Instant** — the single most durable idea
  in the project; still shipping.
- **Material texture over flat colour** — *"CSS backdrops using subtle 'denim
  grain' or 'leather texture' noise rather than flat hex colors."*
- **Anti-cliché contract**, arrived at independently: *Cowboy Clichés*, *Shopify
  Sameness*, *Motion Fatigue*.
- **"…timeless design from the heart of Texas"** — a Texas identity claim that is
  **not** a manufacturing claim. Safe under OQ-04 and worth re-adopting verbatim.

**Superseded later.** The three-direction fork (V2 consolidated); Libre Caslon
(V2 → Playfair); separate desktop/mobile direction pairs; "Grid" as the third
mode label (V3 → "Instant").

**Still missing in production.** The Reject discipline as a living practice.
The Dallas skyline — the only verified real geography in the entire corpus, and a
genuine loss: it grounded Texas in a real place rather than an invented ranch.

**Must remain historical only.** `FOR HER | FOR HIM | BUILT BY YOU | WHOLESALE`
and the *"man in a denim jacket"* hero. **This is where menswear entered the
project** and it must not re-enter. The **circular concho** — origin of the coin
geometry the buckle contract explicitly rejects. The $495 / $285 price band
against verified wholesale $7–$33.

## V2 — Synthesis

**Original purpose.** Collapse three directions into one journey. Eight frames,
end to end.

**Prompt intent.** Prove the story could run continuously from cinematic opening
to wholesale operations without changing visual language.

**Visual vocabulary.** **Playfair Display + Chivo + JetBrains Mono** — V2 set the
type system V3 still uses. High-contrast white headlines over dark portraits.

**Important screens.** Frame 1 cinematic opening · Frame 2 contact-sheet
transition · Frame 4 **Midnight Rodeo** · Frame 5 PDP + customisation ·
Frame 6 wholesale + owner intelligence.

**Strongest concepts — four places V2 is measurably stronger than V3.**
1. **Escape-hatch parity.** *ENTER THE FRONTIER* and *SKIP TO SHOP* as equal
   buttons. V3 demoted skip to small grey text.
2. **MSRP alongside wholesale.** `$185 WHSL / MSRP $395` — ~2.1× keystone. Margin
   maths is the buyer's core decision and **V3 removed it entirely**.
3. **Operational owner intelligence.** A sourcing delay with quantified impact; a
   photography gap with a SKU count and an *Assign* action.
4. **A complete campaign.** Midnight Rodeo with craft story, named chapter, three
   linked products. V3 Frame 7 strips it to ~75% empty black.

**Two mechanics worth recovering outright.**
- **Frame 2 annotates every contact-sheet cell with its motion** — *ENTER: LEFT ·
  ASSEMBLE: DOWN · RECEDE: UP · ANCHOR: BASE*, and on the centre cell **"EXPAND:
  BECOME FILM"**. A real motion spec, in CSS Grid, with stronger continuity than
  V3.1's whole camera passage because transitions are *stated*.
- **Frame 5 puts customisation inside the PDP**, in 2D, with an explicit lead time
  and human-review gate. Simpler and more honest than a separate Atelier route —
  and it confirms **Product Anatomy's origin is film, not 3D**.

**Must remain historical only.** V2 began the fabrications: *"vintage shuttle
looms"*, *"Sourced ethically, milled for resilience"*, *"Hand-tooled in the
Frontier House workshop"*, and the invented customer **"J.D."**

## V3 — Approved platform authority

**Original purpose.** Twelve complete surfaces — the parts a business runs on.

**Visual vocabulary.** Playfair Display + **Hanken Grotesk**. Raised white panels
on a warm ground, hairline rules, label/value commerce rows, outlined controls
with solid black reserved for the single primary action, dark footer with centred
wordmark.

**Important screens.** F1 Ignition · F2 Living Contact Sheet · F3 Image Becomes
Film · F5 One West, Four Worlds · F6 Definitive Flagship Homepage · F7 Midnight
Rodeo · F8 Garment Portal & Product Anatomy · F9 Custom Atelier · F10 Living Cart
& Frontier Passport · F11 Wholesale Showroom & Owner Operating World · F12 Native
Mobile.

**Commerce intent — the finding that matters most.** **The wholesale mechanics
ARE designed**, under different labels than expected: F11 renders *WHLSL Price*,
*MOQ*, *Pack Breakdown* with real size runs (one is **(6)**, matching the verified
prepack), *SKU*, *Add to Order*, *Export Line Sheet*, *Wholesale View*, *In
Stock*, *Waitlist*. **What is missing is the authorisation layer** — there are
zero forms, zero sign-in affordances and zero authenticated states across all 48
exported files. A faithful implementation would leak buyer pricing by default.

**Media requirements.** All imagery is remote, generated and hosted outside
project control. **There are zero production assets** — no GLB, glTF, video,
texture, local font or GLSL anywhere.

**What must survive.** The twelve-surface platform composition; the panel/rule/
label-value system; wholesale as a first-class world; the escape hatch on every
cinematic surface.

**Must remain historical only.** F5's `FOR HIM`; F6's male hero and **$850 men's
boot**; F12k's *"Men's Collection"*; F11's men's workwear rack; the *Kuroki Mill*
and *Leon & Tuscany* attributions (they contradict verified vertical
integration); *Arthur Pendelton*, *E. Vance Holdings*, *J.R. Cash*; the
$45–$1,250 fixture prices; **"EST. 1865" engraved into artwork**.

## V3.1 — Approved cinematic supplement

**Original purpose.** Extend V3 with cinema, space, 3D, mobile and fallbacks.
Never override V3's platform or commerce decisions.

**Strongest concept — and the strongest single idea in the entire corpus.**
The **Frontier Thread → Impossible Warehouse → Belt Buckle Aperture** narrative
encodes the brand's **verified vertical-integration chain** (Textile → Design →
Manufacturing → Warehouse → Distribution → Boutique → Customer) into the geometry
of a navigational object. It is grounded in verified brand truth rather than
invented, which is exactly what makes it usable.

**Mobile intent.** A 14-frame native mobile system with a mode selector, a
reduced-motion journey and explicit fallback states — **none of which V3 had**.
Mobile as an original composition, not a compressed desktop.

**Taxonomy.** **Frame 8F carries the most brand-accurate taxonomy in the corpus** —
Women · Plus · Girls · Accessories & Home · Wholesale, with no menswear. Plus
already has full visual parity with Women; the open question is structural, not
one of prominence.

**Must remain historical only.** The exported Three.js is **broken** — a nested
IIFE resolves a container ID that does not exist and throws on its first line —
and it builds concentric rings while the board and written spec both specify a
**chamfered rectangle**. Do not start from the code. The route map does not
resolve: every route is an unsubstituted `{{DATA:SCREEN:…}}` placeholder. The
wheel-to-camera mapping is scroll interception and is overruled. The tier model
has no Tier 0 — it is a layer stack, not a capability ladder.

## Verdict — what carries forward

| Generation | Contributed | One thing to recover | One thing to keep buried |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **V1** | The strategy | Material texture over flat colour | `FOR HIM` and the menswear premise |
| **V2** | The architecture | MSRP beside wholesale price; stated motion on the contact sheet | Invented sourcing and invented people |
| **V3** | The platform | Escape-hatch **parity**, not a grey link | Menswear, footwear, fabricated heritage |
| **V3.1** | The signature | Tier 0 — a real no-GPU floor | The exported Three.js, in its entirety |

**The substrate nobody chose.** All three Stitch design systems are Material
Design 3 token sets whose base colours drift `#fcf9f5` → `#fcf9f8` → `#fdf8f8` —
**none is Bone White**. The warm western palette has never been the *implemented*
palette in any generation; it lives in the prose layer only. This is a tooling
artefact to correct, not a design decision to respect — and correcting it is what
`src/app/tokens.css` already does.
