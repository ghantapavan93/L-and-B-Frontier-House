# L&B Frontier House V3.1: The Frontier Engine - Design Specification

## Project Intent
A supplemental production study focusing on the signature 3D navigation, motion grammar, and the "Frontier Engine" opening sequence.

## Brand Foundation
- **Core Identity:** Textile → Design → Manufacturing → Warehouse → Distribution → Boutique → Customer.
- **Audience:** Women, Plus, Accessories/Home, Girls, Wholesale.

## The Visual World: "Impossible Warehouse"
A spatial machine that transforms industrial operational elements into high-fashion editorial frames.
- **Palette:** Obsidian Black (#050505), Carbon (#121212), Warm Bone (#F5F2EE), Deep Indigo (#1B2B45), Weathered Leather (#5E4033).
- **Accents:** Electric Cobalt, Hot Magenta, Molten Amber (portal/transition lighting).

## Motion Sequence: "Thread to Trade"
1. **Ignition:** Single luminous thread stitches the L&B mark.
2. **Unfold:** Thread draws a 3D blueprint that expands into the Impossible Warehouse.
3. **Orbit:** Product worlds (Denim, Women, Plus, etc.) orbit the central warehouse floor.
4. **Portal:** Camera passes through the Belt Buckle Aperture.
5. **Resolve:** Spatial rails become the Living Contact Sheet.

## Experience Modes
- **Cinema:** Full WebGL/Three.js choreography.
- **Balanced:** Optimized GSAP motion with 2.5D fallbacks.
- **Instant Shop:** Static posters with immediate catalog entry.
